# digimon
explore a vast world of digimon. 
Digimon: Digital World Survival is a 3D-style browser adventure game where players explore the Digital World, collect and train Digimon, battle wild enemies, and survive in a world full of dangers and mysteries.

Players begin by creating their own character and choosing their first partner Digimon from a selection of 12 or more Digimon. As they explore different areas, they can discover new Digimon, battle them, and try to recruit them by giving them their favorite foods.

Survival is an important part of the game. Players and their Digimon must manage four needs: health, hunger, energy, and rest. Players can gather resources, build shelters, craft useful items, and create healing supplies to stay alive.

Battles use a turn-based system where players can have two Digimon fighting together. Each Digimon has its own health, attacks, and abilities. Digimon can gain experience, level up, and evolve as they become stronger.

The game also includes missions, treasures, crafting, exploration, changing weather, day-and-night cycles, and dangerous boss battles. Some Digimon can even offer missions that reward players with useful items and automatic restoration of their survival meters.

The main goal is to complete the story, defeat powerful boss Digimon, become a stronger trainer, and help save the Digital World.

But be careful: survival is not guaranteed. If any survival meter reaches zero, the player dies and risks losing their rarest Digimon.

Your adventure. Your Digimon. Your survival.
